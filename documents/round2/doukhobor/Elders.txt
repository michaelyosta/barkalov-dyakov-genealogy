Types 
 
This index contains information about Doukhobor eld
ers and advisors who incited the young men of their
 villages to refuse to bear arms in 1895.  These Do
ukhobor 
elders fell into two categories based on their regi
on of origin: 
 
 
E-E Elizavetpol elders arrested and jailed in Eliza
vetpol prison in June 1895; transferred to Nukha pr
ison, Elizavetpol in December 1895; exiled to 
Yakutsk, Siberia in August 1897; and released in Ju
ne 1905.  
 
K-E Kars elders arrested and jailed in Kars prison 
in June 1895; transferred to Metekhi prison, Tiflis
 in January 1896; exiled to Yakutsk, Siberia in 
August 1897; and released in June 1905.  
 
Note: Doukhobor elders from Tiflis province were ex
empted from the above-noted punishments owing to th
eir domestic circumstances (i.e. they were 
already subject to civilian exile).
