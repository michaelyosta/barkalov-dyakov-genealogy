Types 
 
PR Privates in active service in 1895; sent to Ekat
erinograd Disciplinary Battalion; exiled to Yakutsk
 in 1896 for 18 years; released in 1905. 
PR* Privates in active service in 1895; sent to Eka
terinograd Disciplinary Battalion; submitted under 
torture to serve again in 1895-1896. 
RE Recruits called up for active service in 1896-18
99; exiled directly to Yakutsk for 18 years for ref
usal to serve; released in 1905. 
SP Reservists in inactive service in 1895; imprison
ed for 2 years for refusal to serve; exiled to Yaku
tsk in 1897 for 18 years; released in 1905.
