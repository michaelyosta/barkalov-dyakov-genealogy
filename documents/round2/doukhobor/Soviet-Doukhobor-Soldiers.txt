Soviet Union: (i) the Bogdanovka rayon of the Georgian Soviet

ian Soviet Socialist Republic; and (v) the 
Tselina rayon of 
Rostov oblast, Russian Soviet Socialist 
Republic.  This index is for personal genealogical and historical research only.
